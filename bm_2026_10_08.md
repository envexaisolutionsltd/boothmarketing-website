# Booth Marketing AI Automation Rebuild

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: In implementation

## Objective
Reposition Booth Marketing as a practical AI automation agency for established businesses that know AI matters but do not know what to automate, how to implement it, or where to start.

This is a positioning and conversion rebuild, not a visual rebrand. Preserve the premium dark Booth identity, restrained motion, red accents, typography direction, and existing rose artwork on desktop. The rose remains hidden below 1180px.

## Primary buyer
David is an established business owner, MD, director, or senior decision-maker. He has heard about AI and may use ChatGPT, but does not know where AI belongs inside the business.

Core thought: "I know AI could probably help somewhere in the business. I just don't know where to start."

Booth should never require David to understand APIs, LLMs, RAG, agents, vector databases, n8n, or model architecture.

## Positioning
Category: Practical AI Automation for Business

Hero:
"You keep hearing what AI can do. We show you what it can do in your business."

Supporting proposition:
"We find repetitive work, bottlenecks and missed opportunities inside your business, then build practical AI systems to handle them."

Primary CTA: Find My Automation Opportunities
Secondary CTA: See a Working Prototype

## Strategic rules
- Lead with business problems, not technology.
- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Not everything should be automated.
- Prioritise opportunities by size of prize, speed to value, and implementation cost/complexity.
- Keep human judgement where it matters.
- Never fabricate reviews, results, ROI, integrations, security guarantees, or client claims.
- Clearly label demonstrations and independent concepts.
- Do not use em dashes in user-facing copy.
- Do not add public pricing without a deliberate commercial decision.
- Keep the desktop rose visible at 1180px and above only.

## Prototype system
Create a prototype library based on business problems:
- Lead Qualification System
- Customer Enquiry Assistant
- Automated Follow-Up System
- Document Processing System
- Internal Knowledge Assistant

Each prototype explains:
Problem -> Existing Process -> Automated Process -> Human Role -> Potential Business Value.

No fabricated performance claims.

## Proof
Use:
1. Working prototypes.
2. Clearly labelled independent automation concepts.
3. Genuine previous work when relevant.
4. Genuine client reviews only.

The review component may exist before it is populated. Do not invent filler reviews.

## AI Opportunity Audit
Replace the niche-specific Website Audit with an AI Opportunity Audit.

Ask:
- What does your business do?
- Team size
- What consumes more time than it should?
- Repetitive tasks
- Where leads/customer requests go
- Software currently used
- Biggest operational frustration
- Contact details

The prospect should not need to choose an AI solution.

## FAQ
Single-open accordion covering:
- I don't know what I need automated. Where do we start?
- What exactly is AI automation?
- We already use ChatGPT. What's different?
- Will AI replace our staff?
- What happens if AI makes a mistake?
- What happens to our data?
- Will it work with our software?
- How do we know it is worth the investment?
- How long does implementation take?
- Do we need to replace existing systems?
- What happens after it is built?
- What does it cost?
